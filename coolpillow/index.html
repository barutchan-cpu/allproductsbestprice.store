Frequently Asked Questions

Does CoolPillow stay cold all night? +

The gel is designed to absorb and disperse heat. It will not feel refrigerated, but it can remain more comfortable than foam that traps warmth beneath your head.

Can back and side sleepers use it? +

Yes. The recessed center cradles the head for back sleeping, while the higher side zones help support the space between your neck and shoulder.

Which way should the pillow face? +

Place the blue gel surface facing up. You can try the slightly lower or higher neck edge to find the orientation that feels most natural for you.

Will it feel different from a flat pillow? +

Yes. The contoured shape may feel unfamiliar at first, so allow several nights for your body to adjust to the new support profile.

Can I use a pillowcase? +

Yes. A breathable, stretchy pillowcase will preserve more of the gel surface's cooling feel than a thick or heavily padded cover.

Does it need power, water or refrigeration? +

No. CoolPillow has no electronics, water tank or removable cooling pack. Simply place it on your bed and use it like a normal pillow.

What size is CoolPillow? +

It is designed to fit most standard and queen pillowcases. Check the current product dimensions on the checkout page before ordering.

What if it is not right for me? +

Your order is covered by a 30-day money-back guarantee. Contact customer care and follow the returns policy if you decide it is not the right fit.
